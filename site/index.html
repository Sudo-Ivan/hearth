Self-hosted instant messaging and VoIP for friends, family and small communities.

  • One host, one compose stack: API, realtime gateway, voice, media and admin.
  • Podman-first and rootless friendly, plain Docker works too.
  • Happy behind your existing reverse proxy with CrowdSec on the edge.
